# Refund Policy

## Overview

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If something isn't right,
our refund policy is here to help.

---

## General Refund Window

- All refund requests must be submitted within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- Processing time is **5–10 business days** after approval.

---

## Eligibility by Product Type

### Physical Products

- Eligible for refund within **30 days** of delivery.
- Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags attached.
- A receipt or proof of purchase is required.

### Damaged Products

- Damaged products are eligible for a refund within **7 days** of delivery.
- See the [Damaged Orders](./damaged-orders.md) guide for the full report and
  evidence process.

### Digital Products

- **Non-refundable.** All sales of digital products are final.
- This includes downloadable software, e-books, licenses, and digital gift cards.

### Gift Cards

- Non-refundable once purchased.

---

## Orders Over $500

- Any refund request for an order totaling **$500 or more** requires
  **human agent approval** before it can be processed.
- The customer will be notified that their request is under review.
- A human agent will reach out within **1–2 business days**.

---

## How to Request a Refund

1. Contact support with your **order number**.
2. Describe the reason for the refund.
3. Attach any supporting evidence (photos for damaged items, etc.).
4. An agent or the AI system will review and respond.

---

## Exceptions

- We reserve the right to deny refunds for items that show signs of use,
  wear, or damage caused by the customer.
- Refund requests made after the 30-day window will be reviewed on a
  case-by-case basis but are not guaranteed.
- Promotional or clearance items may have a different refund policy,
  which will be noted at the time of purchase.

---

## Non-Refundable Charges

The following are not eligible for refunds:

- Shipping and handling fees (unless the return is due to our error).
- Gift wrapping charges.
- Express or expedited shipping upgrades.
