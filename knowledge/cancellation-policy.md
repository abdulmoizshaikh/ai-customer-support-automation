# Cancellation Policy

## Overview

We understand that plans change. You may cancel an order depending on
where it is in the fulfillment process.

---

## Cancellation Windows

### Before Shipment

- Orders can be cancelled **free of charge** if the order has not yet
  shipped.
- Cancellation requests are processed within **1 business day**.
- A confirmation email will be sent once the cancellation is complete.

### After Shipment

- Orders that have already shipped **cannot be cancelled**.
- You may initiate a return once you receive the item instead.
- See the [Refund Policy](./refund-policy.md) for return and refund details.

---

## How to Cancel an Order

1. Contact support with your **order number**.
2. Request cancellation with a brief reason (optional).
3. If the order has not shipped, it will be cancelled and a refund issued.
4. If the order has already shipped, you will be informed and offered
   a return instead.

---

## Subscription & Recurring Orders

- Subscription orders can be cancelled at any time.
- Cancellations take effect **before the next billing cycle**.
- You will continue to receive items for any orders already placed.
- To cancel, go to your account dashboard or contact support.

---

## Pre-Orders & Backorders

- Pre-orders can be cancelled at any time before the item ships.
- Backorders can be cancelled at any time before the item ships.
- Refunds for pre-orders and backorders are processed within
  **5–10 business days** of cancellation.

---

## Orders Over $500

- Cancellation requests for orders totaling **$500 or more** require
  **human agent approval** before they can be processed.
- You will be notified if your request is flagged for review.

---

## Partial Cancellations

- If your order contains multiple items, you may cancel individual items
  as long as the order has not shipped.
- Once an order ships, individual items cannot be cancelled separately;
  you would need to return them after delivery.

---

## Refund Timeline After Cancellation

| Status                            | Refund Processing Time |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Cancelled before ship             | 3–5 business days      |
| Cancelled after delivery (return) | 5–10 business days     |

- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method**.
- Bank processing times may add additional days.

---

## Exceptions

- Custom or personalized orders cannot be cancelled once production
  has started.
- We reserve the right to cancel orders due to suspected fraud,
  pricing errors, or product unavailability. In such cases, a full
  refund will be issued automatically.
