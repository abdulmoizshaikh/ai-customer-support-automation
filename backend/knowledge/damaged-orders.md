# Damaged Orders

## Overview

If your order arrives damaged, we want to make it right. This guide
explains how to report damage, what evidence we need, and how the
resolution process works.

---

## Reporting a Damaged Order

### Timeframe

- Damaged orders must be reported within **7 days** of the delivery date.
- Reports made after 7 days may not be eligible for a replacement or refund.

### How to Report

1. Contact support with your **order number**.
2. Describe the damage in detail.
3. Attach the required evidence (see below).
4. Do **not** dispose of the damaged item until the claim is resolved.

---

## Required Evidence

To process a damaged order claim, you must provide:

### Photos

- At least **2 photos** of the damaged item.
- One photo showing the **overall damage**.
- One photo showing the **shipping packaging** (box, envelope, etc.).
- Additional close-up photos of specific damage are helpful.

### Description

- A written description of the damage.
- Whether the item is still usable or completely unusable.

### Packaging Photos

- If the outer packaging was visibly crushed, torn, or wet, include
  a photo of it.
- This helps us determine whether the carrier is at fault.

---

## What Happens After You Report

### Review

- Your claim will be reviewed within **1–2 business days**.
- You may be asked for additional information if needed.

### Decision

Once reviewed, one of the following will be offered:

- **Full replacement** of the damaged item (shipped at no cost).
- **Full refund** to the original payment method.
- **Partial refund** if the damage is minor and the customer prefers
  to keep the item.

### Carrier Investigation

- If the damage appears to be caused by the carrier, we may open
  a damage claim with them.
- This does not delay your replacement or refund.

---

## Orders Over $500

- Damaged order claims for items valued at **$500 or more** require
  **human agent approval** before resolution.
- You will be notified that your claim is under review.
- A human agent will follow up within **1–2 business days**.

---

## Return Shipping for Damaged Items

- In most cases, you will **not** need to return the damaged item.
- If a return is required, we will provide a **prepaid shipping label**.
- Never pay out of pocket to return a damaged item without authorization.

---

## Damaged Items in Multi-Item Orders

- If only one item in a multi-item order is damaged, only that item
  will be replaced or refunded.
- The rest of the order is not affected.

---

## Prevention Tips

- Inspect packages upon delivery before signing for them.
- If the outer packaging is visibly damaged, note it with the
  carrier before accepting delivery.
- Take a photo of the package before opening if damage is suspected.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I get a replacement and a refund?**
A: No, you will receive one or the other. You can choose which
you prefer when filing your claim.

**Q: What if I threw away the packaging?**
A: You can still file a claim, but the resolution process may
take longer. Include as much detail as possible in your description.

**Q: What if the damage happened because of my own mishandling?**
A: Claims for damage caused by the customer after delivery are
not eligible for replacement or refund.
