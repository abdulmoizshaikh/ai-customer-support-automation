# Shipping Policy

## Overview

We ship to addresses within the continental United States. International
shipping is available for select products and destinations.

---

## Shipping Methods & Delivery Times

| Method             | Estimated Delivery | Cost         |
| ------------------ | ------------------ | ------------ |
| Standard Shipping  | 5–7 business days  | Free / $5.99 |
| Expedited Shipping | 2–3 business days  | $12.99       |
| Overnight Shipping | 1 business day     | $24.99       |

- Delivery times begin from the date the order is **shipped**, not the
  date the order was placed.
- Orders placed before **2:00 PM EST** on business days are typically
  shipped the same day.

---

## Order Processing

- Orders are processed **Monday through Friday**, excluding holidays.
- Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed the next
  business day.
- You will receive a confirmation email once your order has shipped.

---

## Tracking Your Order

- A **tracking number** is provided via email once the order ships.
- You can track your package using the carrier's website or app.
- Tracking information may take **24–48 hours** to update after shipping.

### Common Carriers

- USPS — usps.com
- UPS — ups.com
- FedEx — fedex.com
- DHL — dhl.com

---

## Lost Packages

### How to Report a Lost Package

1. Verify the tracking status. If it shows "delivered" but you have not
   received it, check with neighbors or your building's front desk.
2. Wait **48 hours** after the delivery date before reporting, as carriers
   sometimes mark packages early.
3. Contact our support team with your **order number** and a description
   of the issue.

### Resolution Process

- We will open an investigation with the carrier.
- Investigations typically take **3–7 business days**.
- If the package is confirmed lost, we will offer:
  - A **full reshipment** of the order, or
  - A **full refund** to the original payment method.

---

## Incorrect Address

- It is the customer's responsibility to provide a correct shipping address.
- Orders shipped to an incorrect address provided by the customer are not
  eligible for free reshipment.
- If you notice an error after placing an order, contact us **immediately**.
  We may be able to update the address if the order has not yet shipped.

---

## International Shipping

- International orders may be subject to **customs duties, taxes, or fees**
  imposed by the destination country. These are the customer's responsibility.
- International delivery times vary by destination and may take
  **10–21 business days**.
- Not all products are available for international shipping.

---

## Partial Shipments

- If your order contains multiple items, they may ship separately.
- You will receive a separate tracking number for each shipment.
- Partial shipments do not affect your refund or return eligibility
  for individual items.
